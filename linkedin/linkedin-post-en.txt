Your AI workspace needs a control panel. Not another collection of config files. 🕹️

I built AI Control Studio — an open-source Mac app that brings models, connections and tools into one interface, with a little pixel-art personality.

What can you manage?

🔌 Connections: choose a gateway independently for Claude Desktop, Claude Code and Codex, with profiles for local Ollama, LiteLLM, Bifrost and custom gateways.

🧩 MCPs and skills: a searchable catalog with connection and permission prerequisites, plus skill discovery and import from GitHub and public URLs — download and review before installing.

🛠️ Your workspace: manage agents, skills, plugins and hooks, and edit instructions that save as Markdown.

⚡ JEV through OpenRouter: skill suggestions and on-demand decision assistance, with approval before every decision request. When no installed skill fits, optional Tavily discovery finds public sources to review before importing.

🔐 Credentials in macOS Keychain, and an offer to restart affected apps after configuration changes.

The interface keeps configuration, skill discovery and activity separate. Search results are readable, and recent activity is paginated and clearable.

Less JSON editing and environment-variable juggling. More control over your AI workspace.

Open for use, feedback and contributions. What would you want to manage from here?
